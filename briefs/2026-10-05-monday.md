## Copy / Avoid / Gap

- **Copy:** the tri-panel action short with one feature claim, as in [Built for Control](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TamGdP-UyOI) (4.2x baseline, 14,605 views). Re-run the format on the RTR 200 and the RTR 310, since the 160 4V version beat Bajaj's whole N160 week.
- **Avoid:** thin brand-level posts with a text hook. [MotoSoul 6.0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBuocO7Pvrk) ran at 0.1x on 372 views. Bajaj's brand-level and Dominar posts at 0.1x show the same pattern.
- **Gap:** organic 310 content. Apache had one 310 post at 0.7x while 31% of its live ads are 310-front. The only rivals making noise on this front are paid pushes, namely Honda's CB350 spots, so there is no organic leader. The [Customer Track Day](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWAo7BS-ACk) post had the week's highest engagement among 310 posts at 4.38%, which suggests a track-day owner angle is worth a stronger push.
