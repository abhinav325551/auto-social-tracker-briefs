# 90-day baseline · TVS Apache vs the field · 2026-09-28

## Where Apache stands

Apache posted 32 times in the window (2.5/week) — third fastest cadence behind Bajaj (5.1/week) and Royal Enfield (3.4/week). Hit rate on organic posts is 9% (2 of 23 scored), the lowest among brands with a meaningful sample (Honda 25%, RE 21%, Bajaj 12%). Apache's boosted_share is just 3% — it isn't buying reach, but its organic hit rate is thin regardless. Racing_track_share sits at 19%, second only to KTM (86%), so the racing angle is present but not converting into outperformance at the rate KTM or even Apache's own launch beats suggest it could.

Apache's front_mix skews heavy to volume_160_200 (53%) with almost nothing on credibility_300_plus (6%) — only two RR 310 posts all window ([Our superpower? Ultimate performance!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wKFdbt61FG0), [Unleashing the Racing DNA in 1080p](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YxQ9q61xJGE)), both near-baseline (0.9 and 0.8 ratio).

## The two fronts: 160-200 volume war and 310 credibility war

**Volume war (160-200cc):** Bajaj is dominant here by sheer weight — 27 posts on this front vs Apache's 17, plus a genuine launch cycle (N160) generating five overperformers up to 12.3x baseline. Yamaha and Honda contribute smaller volumes (10 and 9). Apache's presence is real but its organic ceiling (4.4x on the RR200 MiniGP short) is modest next to Bajaj's N160 launch spikes.

**Credibility war (310cc+):** Apache is nearly absent — 2 posts vs Bajaj's 20 (Dominar/NS400Z), Royal Enfield's 19 (Himalayan 440, Continental GT 650), and Triumph's 13 (Tracker 400, all paid). This is the front Apache is ceding entirely while rivals build premium narrative equity there.

N160 launch is the story — five organic overperformers (3.6x–12.3x), notably [Introducing The All-New N160](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ztq-McCCJY4) at 12.3x. Per trade press, this heritage campaign generated at least 8 distinct articles.

**Yamaha (Tier 1):** Lowest organic footprint — boosted_share 73%, no scorable organic baseline. The #RInstinct teaser campaign is almost entirely paid pushes (5 of 6 top-viewed posts, engagement 0.02–0.18%). Community/creator stories (R15, XSR155 rides) get real views (700K-1M) but these are also flagged paid_push_likely — treat as media spend, not proof of creative resonance.

**KTM (Tier 1):** Racing_track_share 86% — the highest of any brand, and directly on-theme for Apache's own racing DNA push. But 57% of posts are boosted and it has no scored organic baseline. The KTM Cup Season 4 talent-hunt franchise ([India's License to Race is back](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pu-5RuqrCa4), 35M views, 0% engagement) is paid; the "Behind The Racer" creator episodes are lower-view but genuine creator-share (57%) plays.

**Triumph (Tier 2):** 81% boosted, all-in on credibility_300_plus (81% front mix) via the Tracker 400 "Own The Frame" push — six near-identical paid uploads, all sub-0.01% engagement. This is media weight, not creative proof.

**Honda (Tier 2):** Small sample (10 posts) but best hit rate (25%, 2 of 8). NX200 organic posts both hit 2.4x baseline — worth watching as a template, though volume is too low to weight heavily.

**Royal Enfield (Tier 3):** 44 posts, strong organic hit rate (21%) and the standout overperformer of the whole dataset: [Back Where it Belongs | Himalayan 440](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Gz2AaD_TYI) at 35.6x baseline. The "Wandering Sombarais" travel-documentary series is paid (6 episodes, all paid_push_likely, ratios 65-104x) but shows a format worth noting for idea theft: long-form (1000s+) travel narrative as ad-supported brand content.

**Harley:** Zero YouTube posts in window — no data.

## What over-performed across 90 days, and why

Apache's two overperformers are both launch-themed shorts: [TVS Apache RR200 MiniGP](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFlHkoPTqtk) (4.4x, product_reveal, 54s) and [TuRaceLaga 20 years of Racing DNA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MkzDJEmSQOM) (3.2x, heritage, product_reveal, 16s). Both used product_reveal hooks tied to a milestone/launch moment, not everyday content — Apache's steady-state posting (community rides, festive shorts) mostly sits at or below baseline (0.5–1.4x).

Cross-category, the same pattern holds: overperformers cluster around launches (Bajaj N160, RE Himalayan 440/Continental GT 650) and milestone/heritage moments, not routine posting. Apache's single paid_push — [Tu Race Laga | TVS Apache Series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i5VJ6TsuOuQ) (64.9M views, 0% engagement) — is textbook ad spend: massive reach, zero engagement signal, and should not be read as creative validation.

## Patterns across the category

- Launches reliably beat baseline everywhere they're scored (Bajaj N160, RE Himalayan 440, Honda NX200, Apache's own RR200 reveal). Product news is the one theme type nearly guaranteed to outperform.
- Boosted_share is a major dividing line: Yamaha (73%), Triumph (81%), KTM (57%) are functionally running ad accounts on YouTube, with organic baselines too thin to score. Apache (3%) and Honda (40% but small n) are closer to organic-first, and Bajaj (3% boosted) posts high volume mostly organically — meaning its N160 overperformance is real signal, not just spend.
- Racing_track theme correlates with high creator_share only at KTM (57% creator, 86% racing_track) — Apache pairs racing content with almost no creators (3% creator_share), the lowest in the set.
- Credibility_300_plus content is being fought hardest by Bajaj, RE, and Triumph — three brands, one front Apache has nearly vacated.

## Is anyone out-racing Apache on racing?

Yes, on volume and creator integration: KTM's racing_track_share (86%) dwarfs Apache's (19%), built on the KTM Cup Season 4 talent-hunt franchise and "Behind The Racer" creator series — 57% creator_share vs Apache's 3%. However, KTM's racing content is mostly boosted (57%) with no scored organic baseline, so its "racing wins" are unproven as creative, only as spend. Apache still owns the only two organic racing/launch overperformers with real ratios (4.4x, 3.2x) among home-brand posts. The threat isn't that KTM out-performs Apache organically — it's that KTM out-produces Apache on racing-story volume and features real racers as creators, a format Apache doesn't use at all.

## White space Apache can own

Credibility_300_plus is contested by three brands but none of them own "affordable racing pedigree" — Triumph and Bajaj sell premium/heritage, RE sells adventure/travel. Apache's RR 310 has only 2 posts in-window (both near-baseline) despite the brand's racing-DNA positioning being its stated theme (19% racing_track_share, launch 25% of theme mix). Apache could own "track-proven 310" content by pairing its existing MiniGP/racing assets with the RR 310, a combination it hasn't tried yet.

## Taxonomy sanity check

Bajaj's #MyPulsarStory heritage campaign and Yamaha's #RInstinct teaser campaign are each represented by multiple near-duplicate YouTube uploads (5-6 videos each) — treat as one campaign apiece, not five or six data points, when judging campaign-level effectiveness. Similarly, trade press coverage of TVS Apache's "Tu Race Laga" 7-million milestone spans at least 9 articles but is one campaign; Bajaj's 25-years-of-Pulsar coverage spans at least 8 articles, also one campaign. Instagram/web coverage is coverage-level, not post-level — cite as "per trade press," never as engagement data.

## Copy / Avoid / Gap

- **Copy:** Bajaj's N160 launch sequence paired teaser, spec-reveal, and first-ride shorts organically (5 overperformers, 3.6x–12.3x, e.g. [Introducing The All-New N160](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ztq-McCCJY4)) — Apache's own launch shorts ([RR200 MiniGP](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFlHkoPTqtk), 4.4x) prove the same mechanic works for Apache; run more launch-cadence sequences instead of one-off reveals.
- **Avoid:** Don't read the [Tu Race Laga long-form paid push](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i5VJ6TsuOuQ) (64.9M views, 0% engagement) as creative success — it's ad spend with zero engagement signal, same category as Yamaha's #RInstinct teasers (0.02-0.05% engagement) and Triumph's Tracker 400 pushes (0% engagement across 6 uploads).
- **Gap:** Credibility_300_plus is wide open for an Apache-specific angle — only 2 RR 310 posts all window vs Bajaj's 20, RE's 19, Triumph's 13 — while Apache already has racing_track share (19%) and MiniGP/track assets to combine with the RR 310 that no rival is currently positioning as "affordable track pedigree."
