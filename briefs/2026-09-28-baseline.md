# 90-day baseline · TVS Apache vs the field · 2026-09-28

# Where Apache stands

Apache posts 2.5x/week — third busiest, behind Bajaj (5.1x) and Royal Enfield (3.4x). Organic hit rate is 0.09, the lowest of any brand with meaningful sample size except Yamaha and Triumph (both 0.08). Median short views (15d+) sit at 1,331 — a real baseline, not a campaign artefact. Apache's front mix is unbalanced: 53% volume_160_200, 41% brand_level, only 6% credibility_300_plus. Racing_track share of theme mix is 19%, second only to KTM (86%), which is the brand's clearest point of difference — but it isn't converting into organic hits (hit rate 0.09 despite the racing lean).

The one true viral event in the window, [Tu Race Laga | TVS Apache Series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i5VJ6TsuOuQ) at 64.9M views, has 0.0% engagement — flag it as paid media, not proof creative worked, same treatment as Bajaj's and Triumph's ad pushes below.

## The two fronts: 160-200 volume war and 310 credibility war

**160-200 volume war:** Apache commits 53% of its front_mix here (17 posts) vs Bajaj's 27 posts on volume_160_200. Bajaj is winning this front on both cadence and organic proof: the N160 launch stack posted six overperformers 2.7x-11.2x baseline, e.g. [Introducing The All-New N160](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ztq-McCCJY4) at 11.2x. Apache's best volume-front organic hit is [TVS Apache RR200 MiniGP](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFlHkoPTqtk) (short, 4.4x baseline) — good ratio, small base (5,886 views). Yamaha and Honda are lighter-touch here but Honda's NX200 crossover posts (2.1x baseline twice) show a compact product story can outperform on a near-empty content calendar (0.8 posts/week).

**310 credibility war:** Apache is nearly absent — only 6% of front_mix (2 posts), both organic, no standout ratio (e.g. [Our superpower? Ultimate performance!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wKFdbt61FG0), Apache RR310, ratio 0.9 — a miss). Meanwhile Bajaj runs 20 posts on credibility_300_plus (Dominar), Royal Enfield 19, Triumph 13 (81% of its whole output). Triumph's Tracker 400 push is explicitly paid: [Triumph Tracker 400: Own The Frame](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YMSstdfbGFk) at 7.3x ratio, 0% engagement, 17M views — media weight, not creative win. Royal Enfield's Himalayan 440 launch is the credibility benchmark to beat organically: [Back Where it Belongs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Gz2AaD_TYI), 29.6x baseline, 1.99% engagement. Apache has no answer to this on YouTube in-window.

## What over-performed across 90 days, and why

Apache's two organic overperformers are both racing/heritage stage-reveal shorts: [TVS Apache RR200 MiniGP](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFlHkoPTqtk) (4.4x, product_reveal hook, launch theme) and [TuRaceLaga | 20 years of Racing DNA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MkzDJEmSQOM) (3.2x, heritage theme, product_reveal hook). Both are short-form, both use dramatic stage-lighting reveal visuals, both sit under a minute. Pattern: reveal-style shorts tied to a racing/heritage narrative beat plain product or lifestyle posts (community_ride, festive_topical entries mostly sit at 0.5x-1.1x). The one long-form/paid outlier, [Tu Race Laga](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i5VJ6TsuOuQ), is media spend — exclude from creative learning.

## Patterns across the category

Three things repeat industry-wide: (1) launch-week teaser stacks with near-zero engagement but huge views are standard now — Yamaha's R Instinct, Bajaj's Pulsar Original, Triumph's Tracker 400 all do this; treat all as paid, not proof of message-market fit. (2) Creator-fronted formats consistently post the best engagement-to-view ratio among organic content — KTM (57% creator share) and Yamaha (20%) lean hardest into this; Apache's creator share is just 3%. (3) Heritage/anniversary storytelling (Bajaj's 25 years, TVS's 7-million milestone, RE's Wandering Sombarais) is the dominant theme this window across Tier 1 — milestone marketing is oversaturated, so a fresh Apache heritage beat needs a sharper hook than "years since X."

## Is anyone out-racing Apache on racing?

Yes, decisively: KTM's racing_track share is 86% vs Apache's 19%. But KTM's racing identity is built almost entirely on paid amplification (KTM Cup Season 4 ad push, 26,705.7x ratio, 0% engagement) layered over an organic creator-driven "Behind The Racer" documentary series — cheap, authentic-feeling, low view counts (233-2,564 views) but real engagement (up to 7.26% on [India's Largest Racing Talent Hunt is here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J_kAqU1xqo0)). Apache owns more racing_track share than any other volume-segment rival (19% vs Bajaj's 0%, Yamaha's 7%) but its racing content skews stage-reveal/heritage rather than rider-journey story, and it isn't converting to hits (0.09 hit rate). Apache has racing share; KTM has racing narrative depth via creators.

## White space Apache can own

Nobody in the 160-200 segment is running a creator-led "journey to racer" documentary format at scale — KTM does it but at brand_level for its 390/pricier range, not for volume commuters. Apache's own community_ride content (e.g. [Kochi RTX Riders Conquer the Grit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=slcGpb10cgs), one of only three creator-tagged Apache posts, ratio 1.2x) is underdeveloped versus RE's rider-story density. Given Apache's real racing_track share (19%) and near-zero creator share (3%), the gap is a low-cost, creator-fronted "amateur to racer" or "TRL rider story" series — cheaper to produce than reveal-stage shoots, and matches format Honda/KTM show converts on engagement, not just views.

Also note: many Apache "ratio_vs_baseline: null" entries are recent (Sept 2026) posts not yet past the 15-day scoring window — don't read their absence as underperformance.

## Copy / Avoid / Gap

- **Copy:** KTM's "Behind The Racer" organic creator-documentary format (e.g. [From a commuter to a Racer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G1syR1WS49c), 2.0x ratio) — low-cost, real engagement, fits Apache's existing 19% racing_track share better than stage reveals.
- **Avoid:** Treating [Tu Race Laga](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i5VJ6TsuOuQ) (64.9M views, 0.0% engagement) as a creative win to repeat — it's paid reach, not proof the "Tu Race Laga" message resonates organically; Apache's true organic ceiling this window is 5,886 views ([RR200 MiniGP](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFlHkoPTqtk)).
- **Gap:** Own the 300+cc credibility front — Apache runs only 6% front_mix here (2 posts, no standout ratio) while Bajaj (20 posts), Triumph (81% of output), and RE (Himalayan 440 at 29.6x) fight hard for it; even a modest organic RR310/RTR310 push could claim visible share in a category where Apache is currently silent.
