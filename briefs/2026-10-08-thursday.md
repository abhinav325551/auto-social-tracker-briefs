## Where Apache stands

Apache posted 4 YouTube uploads this window, the most of any brand (Bajaj 2, Yamaha 1, Royal Enfield 1). Three were scored against Apache's own baseline, and one was a hit (hit rate 0.33). Boosted share is 0%, so every number below is organic.

- **The hit:** [Built for Control. Ready for Every Ride. | Apache RTR 160 4V](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TamGdP-UyOI) is a short on the 160-200cc front. It ran at 4.8x baseline with 16,914 views and 1.56% engagement. The format is a tri-panel rider action shot with a Dual-Channel ABS callout.

It has no ratio_vs_baseline, so I can't call it good or bad. Apache's long-form organic median is 4,241 views, but the rule is to judge only by ratio.
- **The misses:** Both community posts sat well below baseline.
  - [Goa "throttle never sleeps" short](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOOTA9iGb4M): 0.5x.
  - [MotoSoul 6.0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBuocO7Pvrk): 0.1x.
  - Both have high engagement rates (4.09% and 6.78%) on tiny view counts. Those percentages are small-sample noise and shouldn't be read as success.
- **Split:** The two product posts both went to the 160-200cc front, and both community posts were brand-level. Apache put nothing on YouTube about the 310 this window.

Coverage suggests one 310 item: Rushlane reports TVS [updated the 2026 Apache RTR 310 variants](https://rushlane.com/2026-tvs-apache-rtr-310-variants-updated-quickshifter-sepang-blue-with-base-12557522.html) with a quickshifter and Sepang Blue (5 Oct). These run the factory-visit and MotoGP Meet & Greet creatives and tie to the YZF-R2, e.g. ["Heroes met their fans..."](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1456379149791853) (Meta, 7 days, one of several near-identical variants). Yamaha has a racing story and is paying to tell it. Apache has the TVS Racing name and isn't using it in content this week.

## Copy / Avoid / Gap

- **Copy:** The tri-panel, rider-in-action, one-feature short format. [RTR 160 4V](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TamGdP-UyOI) hit 4.8x baseline, and Bajaj's feature-led [N160 short](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s0xzuO1qsI8) hit 2.6x. Make the next one a 310 quickshifter short.
- **Avoid:** Teaser-only community shorts with no product or payoff. The [Goa short](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOOTA9iGb4M) ran at 0.5x and [MotoSoul 6.0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBuocO7Pvrk) at 0.1x. Also avoid reading Yamaha's 567,029-view [factory short](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Myr0O1p2wGE) as a creative model: it ran at 0.28% engagement and is a paid push.
- **Gap:** Organic racing and 310 content on YouTube. Racing_track share is 0 for all brands, and Yamaha is the only one spending on racing (5 of its 7 live Meta ads). Apache's own ads are 31% on the 310 front.
